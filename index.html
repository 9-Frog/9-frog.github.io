06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Everything you might be wondering.
Prefer a conversation? Let’s talk on WhatsApp.

Is the website really free?

Yes. We do not charge a separate fee for the agreed design and development of your website. We’re currently offering this to promote VISORA and build awareness of our studio through the websites we create. You subscribe to a monthly plan covering hosting, maintenance and your domain. The monthly amount and service terms are agreed with you before work begins.

What do I pay each month?

Your monthly plan is tailored to your website’s needs. It covers hosting, maintenance and your domain. We explain the scope and total before you commit, so you can make an informed decision.

Can my website really be ready in three days?

For an agreed, focused project, that is our target. The three-day build starts after we have your approved brief, copy and brand assets. Larger websites, custom integrations or additional revision rounds can take longer. We confirm a realistic schedule at the start.

Will you guarantee a Google ranking?

No studio can control Google’s rankings. We build a strong technical SEO foundation: clear content structure, descriptive metadata, mobile usability and sensible indexing. Search performance also depends on your content, competition and ongoing work. We do not promise a specific position or page.

Read Google’s guidance on SEO
Will my website be unique to my business?

Yes. Your design direction, page structure and visual details are shaped around your brand and audience. We build a clear, consistent experience that serves your goals, rather than fitting your business into an interchangeable template.

What do you need from me to get started?

A little about your business, your goals and the pages or features you need. If you have a logo, brand assets, written content or examples you like, bring those too. We help clarify the scope before the build starts.

What happens if my needs change?

We review the request with you and confirm whether it fits your current maintenance scope or needs a revised plan. Domain arrangements, service changes and cancellation terms are explained in your agreement before you sign up.
